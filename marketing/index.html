Who does what

Four roles, each with exactly the access they need.

Admin

Runs the school's setup

Creates classes and subjects, issues login accounts for teachers and students, and has read-only visibility into every exam and question bank.

Teacher

Owns the content

Builds the question bank, assembles exams, auto-fills from the bank or hand-picks questions, and grades theory answers.

Student

Just takes the exam

Logs in, sees only the exams scheduled for their own class, and gets their result once grading is complete.
