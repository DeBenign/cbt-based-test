# PRODUCT DEMO (16:9, ~131s, 14 steps). Save as public/voice/demo/<scene-id>.mp3
Voice: female, calm, instructional.

| Scene id      | Step | Role           | Scene length | Script |
|---------------|------|----------------|--------------|--------|
| setup         | 1  | Admin          | 9s  | Step one: the admin adds classes and subjects, then sets the school's logo and color. |
| onboarding    | 2  | Admin          | 9s  | Step two: upload a spreadsheet of students. Each one gets a username, and a login sheet is ready to print. |
| login         | 3  | Everyone       | 8s  | Step three: everyone signs in, and on first login they choose their own password. |
| curriculum    | 4  | Teacher        | 9s  | Step four: a teacher uploads the curriculum as a PDF, Word or text file, and it is split into topics. |
| aiQuestions   | 5  | Teacher        | 12s | Step five: the teacher picks topics, question types and difficulty. AI writes draft questions with marking guides, and the teacher approves each one. |
| examBuilder   | 6  | Teacher        | 11s | Step six: the teacher sets the class, duration and time window, fills the exam from the question bank, and publishes it. |
| exam          | 7  | Student        | 10s | Step seven: the student sits the exam. The timer runs on the server, answers autosave, and leaving the exam is logged. |
| results       | 8  | Teacher        | 8s  | Step eight: the teacher sees multiple-choice scores the moment time is up, and can export them. |
| grading       | 9  | Teacher        | 8s  | Step nine: theory answers wait in a queue, with the marking guide beside each one. |
| studentResult | 10 | Student        | 8s  | Step ten: students see only their own result, which unlocks one hour after the exam ends. |
| activity      | 11 | Admin          | 10s | Step eleven: the admin can trace every action, review flagged attempts, and reopen or regrade with a reason on record. |
| manualPay     | 12 | Platform owner | 10s | Step twelve: for cash or transfer, the school pays the owner, who records it. Access returns and a receipt is issued. |
| onlinePay     | 13 | Admin          | 7s  | Step thirteen: online payment through Nomba is coming soon. |
| mail          | 14 | Admin          | 7s  | Step fourteen: reminders and reset links arrive by email. |
| outro         | -  | -              | 5s  | Benchmark. Request access for your school today. |
